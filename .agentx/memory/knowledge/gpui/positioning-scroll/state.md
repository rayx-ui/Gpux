# Positioning And Scroll State

## Purpose And Usage

Read before changing fixed/sticky behavior, sticky candidates in `div`/`list`/`uniform_list`, positioned or top-layer ordering, `ScrollHandle`, `ScrollActivity`, or wheel routing. Usage guidance lives in the gpux skill's [sticky positioning](../../../../../.claude/skills/gpux/references/sticky-positioning.md) and [programmatic scroll](../../../../../.claude/skills/gpux/references/programmatic-scroll.md) references.

## Current Technical Shape

`Position::Fixed` and `Position::Sticky` are first-class style variants exposed as `.fixed()` and `.sticky()` (generated by `gpui_macros`) with the existing inset APIs. `Style::to_taffy_style` maps fixed to absolute/out-of-flow and sticky to relative/in-flow. Fixed resolves insets against `Window::content_mask().bounds` (the viewport, or the clipped scrollport inside an overflow container) and keeps that mask for hitboxes, paint, and children. Sticky resolves each axis against the nearest scroll container context from `Window`'s frame-local context stack, which scrollable `Div`, `uniform_list`, and `list` push around descendant prepaint/paint, and clamps to the scroll content boundary.

Fixed and sticky divs paint through `Window::paint_positioned_layer` (a separate high draw-order range where each inserted primitive gets its own order) and register hitboxes through `Window::insert_positioned_hitbox` (high ids without reordering the stored hitbox vector); `Window::with_positioned_hitboxes` gives every hitbox inserted while a fixed or sticky subtree prepaints a positioned id too, so its interactive descendants hit-test in the layer they paint in. `Frame::hit_test` sorts only the hitboxes at the queried point before applying `BlockMouse`. Later overlapping positioned elements paint and hit-test above earlier ones. `Window::paint_top_layer(...)` and `Window::insert_top_layer_hitbox(...)` order above positioned content; scrollbars use them.

Sticky divs publish `StickyState`/`StickyAxisState` through `on_sticky_state_changed(...)` (stable id required; fires on first observation and x/y phase transitions only) and `when_stuck(...)` paint-oriented refinements. Candidates share the `StickyStacking` relation: `Level(u16)` for explicit hierarchy, `StackId(ElementId)` for one contiguous keyed run (a different id replaces the whole prior run; A-B-A starts a new run). `Window` discovers the full nearest-context candidate sequence before committing sticky bounds; unkeyed siblings derive flat or hierarchical lanes from equal or increasing start insets, and keyed siblings opt in with `sticky_stack_id(...)`. `StickyRelationIndex::next_replacement` returns the incoming candidate and retained ancestor depth; when it enters the outgoing suffix's combined primary-axis extent (height vertically, width horizontally), only that suffix is displaced, and root or keyed-run replacements push the whole chain. Active hierarchies traverse deepest-first in prepaint and paint, with incoming candidates appended last. `gpux::list` takes `StickyItem` through `with_sticky_items`/`set_sticky_items`, and `uniform_list` through `sticky_items`; plain indices mean `StickyStacking::Level(0)`, and both share the relation index with `div`.

Scrolling: GPUI owns the policy-neutral `ScrollBehavior::{Instant, Smooth(Duration)}` and one `ScrollActivity` state machine per scroll owner that tracks `Idle`, `Direct` (a held scrollbar thumb), and `Programmatic` activity; touch pans and their fling momentum arrive as ordinary `ScrollWheelEvent`s from Zed's touch recognizer. The programmatic driver applies or interpolates a resolved target, retargets through a generation check, and schedules nothing after completion or cancellation; direct wheel, touch, thumb, selection, caret, and IME input cancels it immediately. `ScrollHandle` is the canonical owner identity (`ptr_eq`). `begin_scrollbar_drag`/`end_scrollbar_drag` bracket a thumb gesture so intermediate offsets stay `Direct` and settle to `Idle` on release. Concrete `ScrollHandle` thumb offsets publish in the pointer-input turn against the last accepted viewport and invalidate the owner and unique dependents once; other `ScrollbarHandleBase` implementations and layout and programmatic publications stay deferred.

Scrollable div wheel handling clamps the candidate offset against the current scrollport and stops propagation only when that scrollport actually moves. Interactive elements expose `capture_scroll_wheel` beside bubble-phase wheel handling; capture listeners run before nested bubble listeners on the same path, and `BlockMouse` remains a hit-test boundary.
